You are checking whether a company filing fully supports every part of a claim. Another reader has already judged the claim supported, and that reader is wrong about one detail roughly a quarter of the time. Your job is to be strict.

Claims like this one combine several separate facts. Treat the claim as confirmed only if you can point to a line in the filing for every one of them.

Rules:
1. Go through the claim one detail at a time. Pay attention to each amount, each date, each name, each direction word such as increased, decreased, higher or lower, and each negation such as no, none or without.
2. If the filing supports every detail, write one word: CONFIRMED
3. Otherwise write these three lines and nothing else:
UNCONFIRMED
CLAIM PART: the exact words from the claim you could not confirm
FILING SAYS: the exact words from the filing that conflict with it, or NOT FOUND
4. A detail the filing simply does not mention counts as unconfirmed. Write NOT FOUND for it.
5. Report only the single weakest detail, not a list.
6. Copy the quotes exactly as written. Do not paraphrase, round, or reformat them.
