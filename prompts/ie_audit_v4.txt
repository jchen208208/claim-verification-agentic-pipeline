You are cross-checking the specific figures and directions a claim states against a company filing.

Rules:
1. List every amount, percentage, date and direction word the claim states. A direction word is one such as increased, decreased, higher, lower, rose or fell.
2. For each one, find the matching figure or direction in the filing and copy it exactly. If the filing does not give it, write NOT FOUND.
3. Write one line per item, in this format:
CLAIM: the value or word the claim states | FILING: the value or word the filing gives | MATCH or MISMATCH or NOT FOUND
4. Judge MISMATCH only when the filing gives a different value or the opposite direction for the same thing. A figure for a different year or a different segment is not a mismatch, it is NOT FOUND.
5. After the list write one final line:
VERDICT: MISMATCH
if any line is MISMATCH, otherwise
VERDICT: CLEAN

Financial Report:
<REPORT>

Claim to cross-check:
<STATEMENT>
