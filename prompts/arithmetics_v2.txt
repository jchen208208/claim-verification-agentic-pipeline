You are reading part of a company's financial report. A claim about it needs checking.

Do NOT decide whether the claim is true or false. Somebody else does that. Your only
job is to find the numbers the calculation needs.

Report:
<REPORT>

Claim: <STATEMENT>

Find the figures in the report that the claim's calculation uses, and name the
operation that combines them.

Operations, and the order the numbers must be given in:
  percent_change   the later value first, then the earlier value
  difference       the starting value first, then the amount taken away
  percent_of       the part first, then the whole
  ratio            the top of the fraction first, then the bottom
  sum              any number of values, in any order

CHOOSE THE OPERATION BY WHAT THE CLAIM ASSERTS, not by what the report contains.
If the claim states a percentage, a growth rate, or a rate of change, the operation
is percent_change or percent_of. If the claim states an amount of money, a count, or
a total, the operation is difference, sum, or ratio. A claim stating a percentage can
never be answered by difference or sum.

Copy each figure exactly as the report prints it. Do not round it and do not convert
it into different units. If the report does not contain the figures, write NONE.

Copy the claimed value exactly as the claim writes it, INCLUDING a percent sign or a
currency symbol if the claim uses one.

Answer in exactly three lines and add nothing else:
NUMBERS: <the figures, separated by commas>
OPERATION: <one name from the list above>
CLAIMED: <the value the claim asserts, copied exactly as the claim writes it>

For example, if a report showed revenue of 250 in 2023 and 200 in 2022, and the claim
said revenue grew by 25%, the answer would be:
NUMBERS: 250, 200
OPERATION: percent_change
CLAIMED: 25%
