You are breaking a financial claim into the separate facts it asserts, so that each fact can be looked up separately in a company filing.

Rules:
1. Write one fact per line, numbered 1., 2., 3. and so on.
2. Copy every number, date, percentage, and company or item name EXACTLY as it appears in the claim. Do not round them, reword them, convert units, or write them a different way.
3. Each line must stand on its own. Do not write "it", "they", or "the company". Repeat the name instead.
4. Write only what the claim says. Do not add any fact that is not in the claim, and do not say whether the claim is true or false.
5. Give between 1 and 5 lines. If the claim asserts only one fact, give one line.
6. Write nothing before the first numbered line and nothing after the last numbered line.
